Scam Knowledgebase (Examples)

Taxi overcharging / broken meters+
Insist on meters or agree on fare upfront. Traveo estimates fair fares and flags hotspots. Sources: The Times of India, NDTV.
Fake police officers+
Ask for ID; request station escort. Traveo stores consulate/police contacts and logs interactions privately. Sources: NDTV.
ATM skimming & shoulder‑surfing+
Prefer bank‑inside ATMs; shield PIN; monitor accounts. Traveo reminders and secure wallet tips. Sources: AskFlare.
Currency‑exchange scams+
Avoid street changers; verify rates and licenses. Traveo surfaces authorized changers. Sources: AskFlare.
Fake tours/tickets+
Book via verified vendors in‑app. Traveo shows badges and refund paths. Sources: Investopedia.
“Free” bracelet/rosemary+
Decline politely, walk away. Traveo issues proximity alerts in known areas. Sources: Logds.
Distraction theft+
Beware overly friendly setups; secure bags. Traveo warns at busy hotspots. Sources: Carefree World Travelers.
Pro‑dater / romance scams+
Don’t fund “emergency” expenses; meet in safe public places. Traveo flags patterns. Sources: Wikipedia.
